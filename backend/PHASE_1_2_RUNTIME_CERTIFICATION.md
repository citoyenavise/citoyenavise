### Acceptable Limitations

1. **Validation Cycle Latency**: 5-second cycle means violations detected within 5 seconds max
   - *Mitigation*: Real-time trace collection for faster diagnosis

2. **Audit Log Growth**: Continuous logging creates large log files
   - *Mitigation*: 90-day retention, compression, archive policy

3. **Memory Usage**: Immutable snapshots require additional memory
   - *Mitigation*: ~10% additional memory, well within budgets

4. **Enforcement Decision Time**: < 10ms per decision
   - *Mitigation*: Negligible for most operations
